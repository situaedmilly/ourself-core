# HBC-IDENTITY-01 · v0.1 CANDIDATE

**Standing:** CANDIDATE

1. Matter may exist prior to global recognition.
2. Local distinction may precede global registration.
3. Addressability may precede global identity authority.
4. An `addreSELF` defines a resolution relationship under a namespace; it does not imply canonical identity.
5. Witness establishes historical observability; it does not automatically establish identity.
6. Evidence supports continuity claims; it does not automatically establish an identity verdict.
7. Reconciliation is first-class Relation-Matter.
8. Reconciliation does not rewrite historical Matter.
9. Global recognition does not retroactively create existence.
10. Continuity, addressability, recognition, canonicality, and authority are independent dimensions.
11. `SAME_CONTINUING_ENTITY` requires evidence-bound adjudication.
12. Competing continuity claims may remain unresolved without deleting either lineage.

## Governing axiom

> Recognition changes the standing of an identity; it does not manufacture the history from which that identity emerged.

## Hard non-identities

`EXISTENCE != REGISTRATION`  
`IDENTITY != IDENTIFIER`  
`IDENTIFIER != addreSELF`  
`addreSELF != PROOF`  
`RECONCILIATION != RENAME`  
`RECONCILIATION != ALIAS`  
`RECONCILIATION = EVIDENCE_BOUND_RELATION`  
`RECONCILED != CANONICAL`  
`RECOGNIZED != AUTHORIZED`
