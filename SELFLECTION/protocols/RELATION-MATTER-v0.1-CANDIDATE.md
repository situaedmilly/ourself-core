# Relation-Matter · v0.1 CANDIDATE

A relation is an addressable institutional object distinct from its endpoints. The existence of both endpoints does not establish the relation.

## Minimum relation object

- `relation_ref`
- `relation_type`
- `source_endpoint_ref`
- `target_endpoint_ref`
- `asserted_at`
- `assertor_ref`
- `evidence_refs[]`
- `witness_refs[]`
- `adjudication_ref`
- `standing`
- `contradiction_refs[]`
- `supersession_refs[]`

## Core laws

`MatterIdentity != EndpointRole`  
`NodeExists != EdgeExists`  
`BothNodesExist != RelationEstablished`  
`RelationAsserted != RelationWitnessed`  
`Relationship != ItsEndpoints`  
`RelationMatter != EndpointMatter`

## Reconciliation species

`SAME_CONTINUING_ENTITY` is a reconciliation relation species. It MUST be evidence-bound and adjudicated. It MUST NOT mutate the historical bytes or earlier addresses of either endpoint.
